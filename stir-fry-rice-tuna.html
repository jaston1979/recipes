Stir Fry Rice & Tuna

5 and 1

Source: Tyler Reed

Ingredients

1/2 cup fresh green beans

1 cup cauliflower rice

2 tsp minced garlic

1 tsp ground ginger

1 tsp cardamom

2 tbsp soy sauce

1 egg

Little bit of salt & pepper

Directions

Squirt of Pam in a pot/pan over medium-high heat and then saute green beans. When they start to blacken just a bit add the minced garlic and saute for about another minute. Add pepper, ground ginger, and cardamom. Throw in cauliflower rice and mix well. I add about 1/4 to 1/2 cup of water to the cauliflower rice to soften it a bit. Let the water cook out completely while stirring occasionally. When the water has cooked out add the soy sauce, stir and then create a little “hole” in the middle of the pan for the egg. Spray again with pan and crack and and put in. Break yolk and cook egg while stirring for a bit and then start to mix the egg in with the rest of the rice. Remove from heat immediately after egg finishes cooking.

Then just sear your 4-5 oz tuna to whatever doneness you like and serve together. It’s super good, especially if you’re craving an asian flavor. You could substitute or add any asian spice you like as well.
