Hibachi (Blackstone)

Source: thehungryhussey.com

Ingredients

For Yum Yum Sauce:

1 cup Duke's mayonnaise


1 tablespoon ketchup


2 tablespoon sugar


1 tablespoon paprika


2 tablespoon heavy (whipping) cream


1 tablespoon rice wine vinegar


2 tablespoons melted butter (unsalted) - got this tip from Jason Farmer , thanks Jason!


**Rice: **

2 cups jasmine rice


2 tablespoon chicken powder


2 cups water (roughly 1:1)



Griddle Butter :

1 ½ sticks of softened butter


2 tablespoon garlic paste


1 tablespoon soy sauce


Hibachi:

1 red peppers, diced


1 yellow onion, peeled and chopped


3 green onions, chopped (white part)


1 zucchini, sliced lengthwise


8oz mushrooms, sliced


2 rib-eye steaks


3 chicken breasts


2 tablespoons soy sauce


A dash of white pepper


2 large eggs


Directions

Cooking Rice:
Before cooking, rinse the rice thoroughly to remove excess starch.

To cook the rice, add an appropriate amount of water in a rice cooker and include some chicken powder for a flavorful boost.

Take a tray or pan, and spray it with oil.

Once the rice is cooked, spread it out on the tray and refrigerate it overnight. (it ensures it fries vs steaming the rice)

Preparing Vegetables:
Vegetable preparation mainly involves dicing and cutting.

Dice red pepper and yellow onions, chop green onion and slice mushrooms and zucchini.

Yum Yum Sauce:
Combine griddle butter ingredients.

Mix Yum Yum sauce and taste to adjust flavors as per your preference.

Cooking Vegetables:
Pour some oil on a pre-heated griddle and place zucchini, onions, and mushrooms in three spots on the griddle.

Let the veggies cook for three minutes, then flip everything and cook for an additional three minutes. After cooking, move all the veggies to a cutting board.

Preparing Chicken and Steak:
Salt the chicken and steak, place them on the griddle, add pepper, and cook each side for about 2 minutes.

When the chicken and steak are still undercooked, remove them from the griddle and set them on the cutting board.

Final Steps:
In the final step, add more oil to the griddle and place the rice. While the rice is cooking on one side, put all the veggies on another spot and cook for a few minutes. Add salt to vegetables and mix with the rice.

After mixing rice and vegetables, add soy sauce, a tablespoon of griddle butter, and white pepper. Move rice to one side, scramble the eggs on the griddle, and mix them with rice.

Slice the undercooked steak and chicken into bite-sized pieces and add them back to the griddle separately. Place zucchini on another spot of the griddle.

Add mushrooms and part of white onions to the steak and the rest of the onions to the chicken.

Add a heaping tablespoon of griddle butter to the steak, chicken, and zucchini. You can also add soy sauce to the steak as per your preference.

Cook all chicken, steak, and zucchini separately on the griddle. Now take a large plate and separately add rice, chicken, steak, and zucchini. Serve this mouthwatering Hibachi with irresistible Yum Yum sauce.
