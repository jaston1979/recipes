grandma's fudge

Fudge

Difficulty: Medium

Source: enrichment

Ingredients

In a large heavy aluminum or cast iron (not steel) pot, mix:

2 cups granulated sugar

1 cup cream cheese

1/2 cube butter or margarine

2 tablespoons corn syrup cook and stir constantly over medium heat until it comes to a boil. Continue to boil, stirring occasionally, for seven minutes. Turn off heat, but leave on burner.

add:

1 teaspoon vanilla

1 bottle (about 7.5 oz) marshmellow cream

1 12 oz packet of chips real bittersweet chocolate, vanilla, butterscotch, or mint (mint at bowmans).

Stir until smooth. Beat (on medium or with a spoon lifting really high) until thick and turn dull.

Put in buttered pan and let set. (tin foil in pyrex pan, butter the tin foil).

Cut into squares
