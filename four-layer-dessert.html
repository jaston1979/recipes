four layer dessert

Desserts, Pies

Difficulty: Easy

Source: february dinner hour

Ingredients

crust

1 cup flour

1/2 cup melted butter

1 oz. chopped nuts

2 tablespoons sugar

press lightly into 9x13 pan. Bake at 375 until lightly brown

8 oz. packet of cream cheese

1 cup powdered sugar

8 oz carton cool whip (thawed) mix cream cheese with powdered sugar. Beat until fluffy. Fold in cool whip. Spread over crust

2 3.9 ounce pkg chocolage instant pudding

2 1/2 cups milk mix until thick spread over cream cheese mixture

8 oz carton cool whip (thawed) spread over pudding mixture. Grate chocolate over cool whip. Keep refrigerated.
