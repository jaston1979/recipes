Banana Pudding

Difficulty: Easy

Source: Magnolia Bakery

Ingredients

1 14 ounce can sweetened, condensed milk

1 1/2 cups ice cold water

1 3.4 ounce pkt instant vanilla pudding mix

3 cups heavy whipping cream

1 12 ounce box Nabisco Nilla wafers Don't use generic

4 cups sliced ripe bananas

Directions

In a small bowl use medium speed to beat together the sweetened condensed milk and water for about 1 minute.

Add the pudding mix and beat well for 2 more minutes.

Cover and refrigerate for 3-4 hours or overnight. (If you don't want to wait, you can put it in the refrigerator for 15 minutes and then the freezer for 15 minutes. Don't overdo the times on this quicker version or it won't work). Pudding mixture needs to be set.

In a large bowl use the medium speed on your beaters to whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the pudding mixture into the whipped cream until well blended. There shouldn't be any streaks of pudding in the mixture.

Take a small number of wafers and crush them for garnishing later.

Select a large wide 4-5 quart bowl, glass works best. Arrange 1/3 of the wafers to cover the bottom of the bowl. Place 1/3 of bananas as a layer on top of the wafers. Place 1/3 of the mixture on top of the bananas. Repeat layering 2 more times (total of 3 layers).

Garnish top of entire mixture with smashed wafer crumbs.

Cover tightly with plastic wrap and allow to chill in refrigerator for 4 hours (up to 8 hours). 4 hours works, but 8 hours allows it to set better.
